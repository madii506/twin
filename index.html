    • a pass runs every few minutes for every live twin
    • it reads both prices from Jupiter, measures the gap, and checks the band
    • a new band step means: trim the leader, then buy the laggard
    • then it waits for the gap to open another step, or to close

    No black box. The engine runs one small rule, the same one you replayed in the lab, with hard limits: a cap on the SOL it puts to work, a reserve for fees, a skip on any swap with too much price impact, and a log line for every decision. the full engine pass →

    No wash trading. A twin's maker never trades against itself. It only buys the coin that's behind and sells the one that's ahead.
    
      
    Steps, not spam. It acts once each time the gap opens a new band step, then waits until the gap widens again or closes.
    
      
    Guard rails. Max per trade, a price-impact limit, a SOL reserve for fees, and a {MAX} SOL cap per maker.
    
      
    Your SOL goes home only. Withdraw sells both coins and sends every lamport to the owner's wallet. Nothing else can.

    • the honest answers
    
    
    
      
    What is TWIN?
    Bind two coins to one market maker. When one runs ahead of the other, the maker trims the leader and buys the laggard, so the two charts move together.
    
      
    Where does the maker's SOL come from?
    From you. You fund a maker wallet that TWIN creates for your twin, and you can add more or take everything back whenever you want.
    
      
    Who holds the maker wallet?
    The TWIN engine signs its trades, so it holds the keys while the maker runs. The wallet is public, every trade is on chain, and withdrawals can only ever go to the wallet that created the twin.
    
      
    Can it lose money?
    Yes. Buying the coin that's behind can mean buying a coin that keeps falling. The band, trade sizes, impact limit and the per-maker cap limit the damage. Never fund it with SOL you need.
    
      
    Is the Sync Lab replay a promise?
    No. It runs the same rules over the last day of real candles. Real fills, fees and slippage will differ.
    
      
    What does it cost?
    TWIN takes no fee. The maker pays normal Solana fees and the swap's price impact.
